How I operate

Program leadership with a builder’s mindset.

I’m at my best where operations, program management, systems thinking, and AI overlap — especially when the problem is ambiguous and the existing process is held together by meetings, spreadsheets, and heroics.

My approach

Build the system, not the dependency.

I look for the constraint first: what is actually slowing the work down? Then I design the smallest operating system that changes the outcome — clear ownership, better information flow, reusable processes, automation where appropriate, and a feedback loop.

AI is a force multiplier in that toolkit. I use it to accelerate synthesis, classification, drafting, pattern detection, and workflow execution — without handing away accountability or judgment.
