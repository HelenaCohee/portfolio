WHY PARK SOMETHING

Saying no is part of the job

AI-driven promotion recommendations are a textbook example of high-risk, low-feasibility work. Bias risk on employment decisions is severe, regulatory exposure is real, and the data infrastructure isn't there yet. Parking it now isn't avoiding the work; it's sequencing it past higher-leverage wins that build trust in the agent first.

PHILOSOPHY

Lightweight, not heavyweight

Governance should accelerate AI work, not slow it. The right cadence makes risk visible early, keeps stakeholders aligned, and prevents the kind of late-stage surprises that erode trust. Built as a partnership with the central AI/Agentic COE, Legal, Security, and PeopleTech.

Weekly · 30 min

Portfolio Sync

P&C AI program team. Status on active initiatives, blockers, decisions needed.

Bi-weekly · 60 min

Risk & Compliance Review

Legal + Security + PeopleTech. New use cases, data access patterns, content review.

Monthly · 60 min

Portfolio Steering

P&C leadership + AI/Agentic COE. Sequencing, sunset decisions, capacity reconciliation.

Quarterly · 90 min

Roadmap Refresh

Full P&C function. Outcomes review, strategic re-prioritization, investment asks.

DECISION FRAMEWORK

Four-question gate before any AI use case ships

1. What's the human-in-the-loop pattern? No employment decisions without human review.
2. What data does it touch, and at what access level? Read-only by default; write access requires explicit governance approval.
3. What happens when it gets it wrong? Defined fallback paths, escalation logic, and content review cycles.
4. What's the sunset trigger? If it's not delivering measured value within 90 days, what's the off-ramp?

PRINCIPLE

Outcome metrics first, then activity metrics

Activity metrics (number of pilots, agents deployed) are easy to game and tell you nothing. Outcome metrics (deflection rate, behavior change, time saved) are what makes the AI story match the success metrics. Tracked weekly, reviewed monthly, presented quarterly.

REPORTING CADENCE

Different stakeholders, different views

Weekly internal: Activity + leading indicators for the program team.
Monthly to P&C leadership: Outcome metrics + portfolio decisions made.
Quarterly to executive leadership: Business impact, sequenced wins, investment asks.
Annual: Full portfolio review, sunset decisions, next-year roadmap.

PHILOSOPHY

Listen first. Ship one thing. Build the system.

The temptation in a new role is to demonstrate value through volume. The actual job is to demonstrate judgment through sequencing. By day 90, the goal is one shipped initiative as proof, the operating system stood up, and the portfolio roadmap published.

Weeks 1-2

Embed and Listen

1:1s with Sr. Director People Operations, P&C function leads, PeopleTech/Engineering, Legal/Security, AI/Agentic COE. Map active P&C AI surface area: primary agent capabilities, in-flight work, content libraries.

Weeks 3-4

Audit and Score

Inventory existing and proposed use cases. Score on the four-dimension framework. Draft v1 of the portfolio roadmap. Identify the one initiative that's both high-leverage and shippable inside 90 days.

Weeks 5-6

Publish and Align

Share v1 portfolio roadmap with P&C leadership. Stand up the governance cadences. Start the bi-weekly Risk & Compliance review. Lock the priority initiative for proof-of-pattern execution.

Weeks 7-10

Ship the Proof

Execute the priority initiative end-to-end with the team. Run the pilot, measure outcomes against the metrics framework, iterate. Document the operating model so the pattern is repeatable.

Weeks 11-13

Scale and Plan

Present 12-month portfolio plan to P&C leadership and AI/Agentic COE. Publish results from proof-of-pattern initiative. Sequence next two priority initiatives. Establish quarterly executive readout cadence.

WHAT THIS ENABLES

By day 90, the portfolio is legible

P&C leadership knows what's coming, what's parked, and why. PeopleTech knows what's on their roadmap. The AI/Agentic COE has a clear partner. One shipped initiative has built credibility. The metrics framework makes future decisions easier. And the operating model means this work continues even when I'm not in the room.
