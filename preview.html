Background

I’m a program manager who builds.

Not just plans, decks, and status reports — actual systems teams can use.

How I operate with AI

Build the system, not the dependency.

Across HBO, Quibi, Netflix, and the work that followed, I grew by solving problems nobody asked me to solve and building things people did not expect a program manager to build.

I use AI as a force multiplier, not to replace thinking, but to accelerate building. I scope the problem, identify the constraint, architect the solution, and ship tools and operating models that solve real pain.

My approach draws from strategic project management, constraint management, influence without authority, change adoption, and systems thinking.
