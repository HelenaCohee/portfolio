Agents + Skills + Governance

The system behind the AI work.

I design modular AI systems: agents orchestrate the work, reusable skills provide specialized capabilities, governance keeps actions bounded, and evaluations measure quality. Public pages show the architecture — not the proprietary implementation.

Fast command layer

Shortcuts are not the same thing as skills.

Shortcuts are lightweight command patterns for changing how the model approaches a task. Skills are deeper reusable procedures with logic, guardrails, inputs, outputs, and evaluation criteria. I use both — and keep the distinction intentional.

Responsible execution

AI proposes. Humans stay accountable.

H

Human-in-the-loop

High-impact actions require human review before anything is sent, assigned, published, or changed.

E

Evidence before confidence

Recommendations distinguish sourced evidence, inference, and uncertainty instead of presenting everything as fact.

D

Data minimization

Workflows are designed to use only the information required for the task and avoid exposing sensitive internal data.

B

Bounded actions

Agents have explicit operating boundaries, escalation paths, and stop conditions rather than unlimited autonomy.

Evaluation

If you cannot measure it, you cannot improve it.

I evaluate agent outputs against dimensions that matter to actual work, not just whether the answer “sounds good.”

Accuracy

Are claims correct and supported by the available evidence?

Completeness

Were important decisions, risks, actions, or constraints missed?

Grounding

Can important claims be traced back to a source or explicit input?

Actionability

Can a person use the output to make a decision or take the next step?

Relevance

Does the output stay focused on the actual job to be done?

Risk & confidence

Are uncertainty, sensitive actions, and possible failure modes represented honestly?
